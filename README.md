# Ghost Patrole - Pain Distraction using Haptic Feedback and Immersive VR

This project investigates the use of a multisensory virtual reality (VR) experience as a
distraction method during blood sampling,
with a focus on reducing perceived pain
in children. A ghost-catching VR game
(Ghost Patrol) was designed and implemented to combine visual, auditory, and vibrotactile feedback while minimizing discomfort and cybersickness. Two custom vibrotactile armbands were integrated into the
experience to provide synchronized haptic
stimulation near the needle insertion site,
to utilize the pain gate theory. To evaluate the results of the integrated vibrotactile
armbands, a controlled within-subject study
was conducted on young adults using a simulated needle prick under three conditions:
no vibrations, hand vibrations, and hand- and arm vibrations. Using participantreported VAS-scores, the hand- and arm
vibrations produced a significantly lower
perceived pain, which was also supported
by a semi-structured interview with each
participant. Findings highlight potential
for synchronized haptic feedback for nonpharmacological pain management. However, further research must be conducted on
children in a clinical setting.

*I also encourage to look at the repository for the [Haptic Armbands](https://github.com/mattiHedegaard/CustomHapticArmbands) used for this project.*
